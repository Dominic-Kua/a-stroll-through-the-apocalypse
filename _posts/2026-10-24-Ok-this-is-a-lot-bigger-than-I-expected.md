## 24th October, 2026


So I set off this morning, bright and early, road was actually clear the whole
way here, so it only took me an hour. Now this is, I think, a regional
distribution hub, but it's sodding massive. Literally bigger than a football
pitch. This whole industrial park is massive though, honestly I could live here
and walk to get anything. I made porridge this morning with the oatmilk, which
is a bit reductive, but hey, it was tasty. Had it with golden syrup in. 

So I've got protein bars, but I got into the warehouse through the loading bays
and there was a lorry there loaded with food and drink. Honestly there's beans,
lentils, rice, pasta, flour, every single fizzy drink I've ever heard of. Even
some of that spice powder I found three doors down. Didn't taste of much, but it
somehow made the flavours of the food really good when I put it on the canned
food.  This stuff has a much stronger smell, but I am glad it's here, I'm nearly
out of the other pack. 

Anyway, I've got four bicycles, a self tanning kit, a daylight lamp (might need
that), and a whole pallet of luxury chocolates here. I am going to eat so well. 

There's sort of places I could stay here, but unlovely is the only adjective for
this area. I won't move here. But I might well learn to drive the lorry,
I should scout for a nice place to live next year. 

I'm alone, but somehow I feel a lot better now than I did even a few days ago.
I've been reading this diary, I think I was ill. So much about animals (there
are none). 

I think the change of food has been good for me, getting out and about. Not just
wallowing in the village, missing my old life. 

Right packing up what I can for now and heading back before dark.
