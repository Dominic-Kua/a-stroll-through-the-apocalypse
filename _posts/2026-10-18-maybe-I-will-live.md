## 18th of October, 2026

Today is better, decided to use some battery power from the house down the
street and have hot food. 100% worth it. Nothing like a Frey bentos pie and some
hot potatoes in a can with hot peas and even a hot cup of tea. Honestly, I need
power more for winter. I should probably start working out where that warehouse actually,
specifically is. Except of course my maps app is... well, no one's updated it
and I can't exactly get on the internet any more. No internet for a start. 

Technically if I find a car with a GPS with that in there, I could use it, but
I'd have to turn on and do a search and hope the local failover has a decent
search and then hope that it's in the cache... it's just too much. So I decided
to go analogue. I'll head to the county hall and see if I can't find the
planning permission department, I bet they keep everything in paper format,
probably in triplicate. So that's where I'm driving tomorrow. 

While I'm in town, I might as well hit up the camping stores, get some gas, some
camping heaters and a cooker and kettle. Keep the power for light and my
electric blanket and I can probably make it through the winter OK. 


Wonder if Father Christmas is still out there. At least I'll be able to see him
in the dark now there's no light pollution.
