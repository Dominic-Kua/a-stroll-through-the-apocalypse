## 25th October, 2026

So driving a lorry, not as hard as it looks. I mean you need to change gear
about once every three heartbeats at first, but when there's nothing on the
road and it's just A-roads all the way? Gravy. 

I found some packs of curry paste and dehydrated meat subsitute at the warehouse
last night, made some pretty spicy curry last night, it was marvellous. You
know, before this happened, I was never one for diaries, but now I really enjoy
it. Remembered that lots of people in the village had chickens, some escaped,
some survived so they live in my shed now. Should have eggs sooner or later,
which will be nice. Plus they will eat my waste from the table. 


Anyway, I got a lorry and I bought it to the warehouse which is where I am at
now. I did npt learn how to reverse a lorry, I did choose an empty lorry, but
now I'm here it'll probably be easier to just find a fuelled lorry with space in
the back and we'll we'll have a forward pointing vehicle that can be filled with
all the things I'll need, probably forever. 

This place is an absolute mess though, no order at all. There's toilets on the
same shelf as sweets. Everything is in the dark, I'm just going to look at the
closest shelves and get what I need. The rest can wait. I've also got a sleeping
bag and I'm making use of the facilities here. Fancy shower for me tonight.
Drive home tomorrow. Not noon yet, I've got hours to hunt today and tomorrow.
