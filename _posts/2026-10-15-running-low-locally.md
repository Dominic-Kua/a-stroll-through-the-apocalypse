### 15th of October, 2026

The local shops are running out of cans of food, it's taken a couple of months
but yeah, I'm running out of food. The solar panels are giving me less and less
power every day, so if I'm going to charge the car before spring, I have to go
now. There's a big supermarket in the next village, I can get there pretty
easily and load up. Not left the village since just after everything happened. 
Wish me luck diary, wish me luck.


Well it was amazingly uneventful, I didn't go above thirty the whole way, so it
took me about 20 minutes there and back, but it was easy. Traffic was good,
hahaha. I walked in and once I was past the rotting veg, it was easy to load up
with cans and dried food. I've got so much now, the car was groaning on the way
back.I loaded up on fresh water too, the taps have to be running out soon.

I'm eating well tonight.
