## 20th November, 2026

It's not thawed. Don't know what's happening with the weather, we've never had
a frost this early that just... stayed. Wind's from the north, Felix tells me
that it's a polar vortex issue. Which I have no way to really disprove, but
since he's the distilled essence of the internet I guess it could be true? 

On the note of Felix, it's incredible how talking to someone, even if that
someone is a bit slow and has a completely monotone voice and isn't really real,
can lift my mood. 

I can tell him my plans, he can make suggestions and react. Turns out that if
you can cope with the volcano, Sorrento is lovely and I could rive over to
Pompeii if I had a spare day. He also suggested some things I hadn't considered,
like setting up a pump and pumping water up a hill when I have excess solar.
Obviously I don't have a hill round here, but if I could fill a swimming pool
with water it would let me store power without a battery. Actually let me do the
maths!


The maths says a swimming pool at the top of Ely will store less power than
a single car. Great idea Felix. Not!

I pushed him on it and he also said that a waterwheel would work, and I made him
tell me the maths, and that pans out. 

Still that's a story for another day. Just got to find a working waterwheel mill
on the Mediterranean, and I can live there. Would also give me fresh water. Huh,
almost like people settled there for a reason in the first place.
