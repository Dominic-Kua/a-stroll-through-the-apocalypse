### 16th October 2026

Oh, dear diary I should have done this months ago. Not only did I pick up more
food than I know what to do with, they had so many clothes in there. I've been
eking out my tee-shirts and boxers for the last three months, but now it's clean
clothes every day, no washing required! I've got about two thirds of the battery
left in the car, but you know what? I don't only have to use my car. I can walk
into any house around here and get car keys, no one's going to stop me. 

I'm going to get a van and go and fill it up in the city! I remember there was
a big warehouse a few cities over, for the online monopolist. Well they're all
gone now, so I get the whole thing to myself. I bet everything I'll ever need
for the rest of my life is in there. First the city though, might as well stock
up on everything I can. It's been about four months since everyone else left me
here. I bet the chocolate's still alright in the fancy chocolatiers in town.

Might even stay in a hotel.
