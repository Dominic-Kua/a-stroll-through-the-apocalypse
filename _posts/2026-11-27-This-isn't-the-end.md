## 27th November, 2026

She always said I was melodramatic. 

She was right, as usual. After a night's sleep in a warm bed, in a house not at
risk of flooding, I remembered something.

Sandbags. I could sandbag up our house and then come back in Spring, clean it
out properly and say goodbye like I meant to.

Emotions are hard to deal with, but Felix helped me. Upside, if I'm feeling
emotions like this, apparently it means I'm processing my grief, and my
survivor's guilt. I told Felix that he was actually a therapist and that's been
helpful so far. 

I'm driving back down in a big off road car I found in a garage. It's comically
high off the ground so I think it can handle any floods easily. I know that the
parish council had sand and bags, in case of a flood coming up the river. So
I'll just put the house to sleep today, clean it up, get it ready for spring. 

Hey, maybe I can even make some permanent flood defences, use it as a summer
house.
