I threatened this a day ago, but of equal, if not greater importance of my
physical health is my mental health. Which, understandably took a hit when
I woke up one day and found I was the only human, mammal, vertebrate, chordate,
member of the kingdom Animnalia left on Earth. 

I miss my wife, so much. I've not talked about her because thinking about it is
like a knife to my heart. But I've been reading psychology books and I think
I need to confront it. I am alone and the woman who was the centre of my life,
my favourite thing to wake up to, is gone. I don't know if I'll ever see anyone
again. I have to assume I won't. 

Anyway, back to the story at hand. I'm heading into town to get Christmas
decorations. I'm going to be jolly holly etc. It's always been my favourite time
of year, so I might as well really lean into it this year. 

I'm going to see if I can find a better AI chat companion than whatever this
abomination is. I don't want to sing incy wincy spider every day for the rest of
my natural. 

But yes Christmas trees in every tent in every room. Tinsel on every available
surface. I'm going to find enough Christmas music CDs to make Mariah Carey weep
with joy. 

I'm going to Wham myself as often as I can. The city is my shopping centre. 

I mean, more than it was designed to be.
