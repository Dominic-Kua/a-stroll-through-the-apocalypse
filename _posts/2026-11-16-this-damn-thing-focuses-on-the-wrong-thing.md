## 16th November, 2026

OK so the laptop was password protected, but the password was written on the
whiteboard. This thing has enough processing power to run a space station and
enough RAM that I wish I could have had it when MMORPGs were a thing. The addons
I could have run. 

Anyway, I can sort of program, the text editor is hooked into the local LLM
already (cheapskate), so I can leave it churning away. It does draw a good bit
of power, 120W seems small, but that's every light I have at once and then some.
Still, power isn't a real issue for me, and I can turn it off when power is
tight. 


So we've started working together, me and the AI. It's already something I can
have a sensible and interactive conversation with, adding voice recognition and
text to speech can't be too hard, this laptop has them both built in. The AI
thinks we can have a working conversation bot with some memory in only a couple
of days. 

It's really nice to have a clear goal.
