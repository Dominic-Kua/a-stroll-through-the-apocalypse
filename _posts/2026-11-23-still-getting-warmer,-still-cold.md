## 23rd November, 2026


OK so it's still below zero, but only just. Only just. One degree below and it's
basically like summer now. The snow stopped, but I expect more to come, the air
feels heavy and the sky is yellowish. I don't have any computer aided weather
reports so I'm going with my gut. Let's see if I'm right. 


Felix got woken up, and I put a heater and a battery in the camper van, parked
that bad boy on my driveway. I'm going to have a shower and a blow dry tonight,
before I fire off the firework. The big thing is I have to be properly dry
before I come back into the tent, Moisture is my enemy. 

That said, this could have been catastrophic, I genuinely could have frozen to
death. If I hadn't made the preparations for the winter, I'd be dead. As it is
I'm cold. I'm grumpy, but I am alive and fine. In bed I'm not even that cold,
I piled every duvet under and over me and kind of made a nest. 

I'm not staying here all winter. Sorry love, I know we bought this house
together but I can't maintain it without utilities. I need to get to somewhere
on higher ground, sheltered and probably near some water. 

You know, Derbyshire is only a few hours away, I could be near a reservoir. It's
only for the rest of winter. Probably need to start planning. Will need to get
out of the village, I think it'll be doable about a week after the thaw. Going
to have to change everything I've planned. I'm heading to the med next summer,
I'll work it out from there.
