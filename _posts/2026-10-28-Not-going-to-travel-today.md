## 28th October, 2026

Well, not going to go home today as planned, a storm's hit and my old empty
lorry got rolled across the car park. It's raining sideways out there and I am
not driving home if I don't have to. The house is locked up so it shouldn't be
a problem, I hope. 

Still even if it's completely gone, I have so much in the lorry now, in its
lovely sheltered loading bay, that I could just carry on and find somewhere new
to stay. I'd want to pick up the photos of my family though. I wonder what
happened to them. 

I wonder what happened to everyone. 


I've been trying to put it out of my mind, but... honestly, why am I all alone? 

I'd be sadder, but I found some very, very good rum so you know what, I'm not
driving so I might as well have a tipple.

Cheers.
