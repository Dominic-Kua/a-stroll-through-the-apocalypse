## 30th October, 2026

Right, there's been a development. That development is that I am bloody awesome.
There were three trees down on the road on the way back, and I handled them all
like a boss. 

That means I am now back at home, I parked up on the main road, no need to bring
it into my street after all. I've been shipping things across all day and my
house is very well stocked now. 

I've got extra insulation for the windows in the form on a film I can put over
the inside of the windows, lets light in, keeps heat in. Honestly the house was
freezing when I got back. I have electric blankets, I have solar panels and
batteries set up all over the road. I will be able to survive this winter
without freezing. I have done the maths, I have enough food, calories and
nutrient wise to take me until May. That's six months supplies from a week's
work.

This is a level of leisure time not seen in millennia. Really though, this
winter will be research. I need to know where I can go, how I can get there and
if I want to dig in or go mobile.

If I can make it to the mainland, I could get a band's tour bus and live in
that, going around the med, at least until the fuel ran out. Or I could find
a good spot for a summer home, maybe a village on a hillside somewhere in the
Loire valley?

I've got at least 6 months to think about it. I also got myself some books on
programming, if I have a laptop maybe I can write a better AI than this cheap
thing? I don't know. Either way I can learn I guess?
