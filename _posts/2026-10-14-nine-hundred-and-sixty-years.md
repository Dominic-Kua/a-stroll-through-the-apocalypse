### 14th of October, 2026

Today marks nine hundred and sixty years since the Battle of Hastings, I think.

The calendar has changed a couple of times since then, but since I'm the ruler
of the world now, I'm declaring it 960 years. I'd have waited until a thousand
years, but I don't think I'll be around then. 

Anyway, it's the anniversary, hurrah. I might even go and see if I can find the
tapestry, it was meant to come to England around when the end happened. No one's
left to care about it but me anyway. 

I remember reading that diaries helped people struggling with loss, but it's not
kicked in yet. I'll give it a go though, see how it helps. 


They're all gone, everyone is gone. There's just me and the animals now.

Do I even want to live alone in the world?
