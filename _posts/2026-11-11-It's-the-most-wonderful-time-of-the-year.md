## 11th November, 2026

It's remembrance day today. I was in town anyway, so I went to the war memorial
and I did the minute's silence. I guess everyone else did it better though,
since you know I've not heard a peep from them in months. About forty seconds in
that sodding AI chat toy asked me if I wanted to sing Baa Baa blacksheep, so
I don't have an AI chat toy any more, but the church has one on its spire.
I might come to regret that, but hey, Cambridge is the city of tech, I'm sure
someone in this place had a more capable model. 

Might require me to check a few thousand houses though. Or wait, the offices of
the firmware guys,I bet the universities have a lot of good stuff. Add that one
to the plan. 

I was in town because I got loads of good decorations yesterday, but what
I didn't get was a live Christmas tree. No chance of a goose this year, or any
year coming I guess. So I've picked out some of my finest cans for Christmas
day, I'll have a feast. I only hope it's warm enough, that's my real worry,
it's getting colder and colder by the day. I half expect snow to show up soon. 

Anyway, I had a mate who kept a tree in a pot outside and brought it in for
Christmas, he wouldn't mind me looking after it.
