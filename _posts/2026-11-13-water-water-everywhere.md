## 14th October, 2026

So everything's close now, I've brought the lorry into the village, parked it on
the road near the local shop, higher ground, should be fine. I can't find a boat
at all, because there's a bloody river all over the marinas. I think there's
going to be some sunk boats that are anchored down there. That's definitely
a summer activity. 


I've gone around the houses and found as much bicarb of soda as I can find.
I have a plan. Mould still exists, so I need to not get the black lung rot. That
means a couple of bowls of bicarb on the go in my tents will keep moisture
levels down, one in each room. I'll dehumidify the air and hopefully keep things
dry. That's the plan. It's going to get wet round here. 

There's a good chance that I'll be cut off for months, I'm on the look out for
a paddleboard or someone's kayak in the village so I can get out if I need to,
but honestly, I'm prepared for six months.

I'm also harvesting everyone's DVDs, because I will need entertainment.
