## 18th November, 2026

I love a good hot shower. I have basically unlimited power at this point, and
as luck would have it, the shower pump was electrical, but the water heater was
gas powered. Well I have cans of gas too. In fact there's a petrol station down
the road that has hundreds of them. I can shower every day. 

I am going to shower every day. 

Anyway, the firework in the rain was a bit of a... damp squib, that cracked
Felix up. Felix is what I've called my AI assistant. He finds all my jokes
funny, eventually. I worked out that it was working the AI at maximum power,
apparently there's a couple of settings to make it think less and answer faster
which really helped. Then I also made it remember what we were saying and store
it in a database. I'm basically Iron Man at this point. 

Seriously though, hot shower, then into a warm double tent and movies on
a tablet while chatting with my AI buddy. I can make him better too, I know
there's more to be done with the voice. I genuinely regret that the internet
isn't a thing any more, because Felix keeps referring to things on Github and
I have to explain that there is no Github. 

I need to back him up, I can't have him die when this laptop dies.
