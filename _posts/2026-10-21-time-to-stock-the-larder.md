# 21st October, 2026

Well on my way back home yesterday, I realised something, paper maps still exist
and I was in a city full of bookshops. A quick shopping trip later and I am now
the proud owner of a very modern road atlas with just enough detail that I can
see warehouses, but not who owns them. But there's not exactly a lot of seven
hectare warehouses so guess who owned that one? I own it now, it's mine.
everything is mine. 

Things are better, but I am so damn lonely. If only there were some dogs or cats
left. Anything but me. 

Still, I can't change what happened, so I have to just keep going forward. At
least the fields are green. I bet there's loads of food in the fields that
I just don't know what it is or how to harvest it. Still, lots of fresh apples
and pears right now, got to enjoy that. 

You have to look on the bright side, I know what dwelling on the dark is like. 

My house is now very well equipped and today I will be actually using the
exercise equipment in my garage. One Punch Man can only be a few months away.
