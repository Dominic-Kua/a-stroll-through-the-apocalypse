# 12th October, 2026

So big developments today. Really put my plans to move out of here into
perspective. This morning I thought it was a nice day and I wanted to go for
a walk. Now in the before time, I would walk by the river, and I loved it, there
were herons, grebe, cormorants, swans, moorhens, coots, kingfishers, red kites,
buzzards, kestrel and I even heard a cuckoo once. The reeds were full of
warblers. 

I miss birds a lot, almost as much as my family. They were the sound track for
a lot of my life. 

So I headed down to the river, crossed over the train tracks and... well there
was the river. I've always known the fens relied on active pumping, and with no
one here to pump it, well... the fields by the river are now in the river, the
whole fens is filing up. We had a very dry summer and I think it's been letting
the ground hold a lot more than it otherwise would have, but... I think I'm
going to get cut off soon. The village has been here at least a millennium so
it'll be fine, but the roads will be flooded probably by the end of winter.
I thought I was being clever by stocking up until late spring, but I need to
bring the lorry up from the bus stop soon. It'll not be accessible soon. 

There's a boat club down the way though, if any of the boats didn't float away,
I should be able to use them. If not... well I'll find a way. Plenty of
warehouses out there, plenty of cars, I can wade or swim to dry land in the
spring. 

Just got to keep up with the damp over winter. The house isn't heated any more
and these new builds aren't like the old stone houses. Relocating to the med
feels more urgent than ever.
