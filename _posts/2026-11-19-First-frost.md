## 19th November, 2026


The first hard frost of the year hit this morning, thermometer outside read -5.
No clue how it's that cold this early in the year, but oh well. 

Guess it's good that there's no water at pressure in the pipes because they're
going to burst. I bet every house out there that I left the door open to will be
flooded. My bad!

That said, the water probably dropped out when the pressure went, might be OK.
It was bloody freezing today outside, I like to step out in the morning, sit on
the porch and appreciate the world with a coffee. Not a good coffee. Actually
I bet the local roastery has sealed bags of green coffee. I could learn to roast
it? Well that's a job for another day. 

I'm a bit scared, it's been fine so far in my tent and covers, but if it stays
cold for a long time, I'm going to need to burn power to heat myself. 

Well, we'll see. Might have to make like the French Shepherds I heard about and
stay in bed for most of the winter. Can't be good for the muscle tone though.
