## 25th November, 2026

Well the thaw came, I can probably get out of here now. I didn't want to leave
here before spring, but I really think that there's going to be a big flood
happening if we get any kind of storm from the East. 


I don't think the road will be passable to the East, South has Cambridge which
is on higher ground, but Ely would have been nice. I plan to go to Peterborough
though, completely safe from the floods, right by the warehouse. I'll be OK to
hole up there for the winter. 

I've packed the essentials, photos, keepsakes. I'm going to say goodbye to the
house that I lived in with her. This was our home. This was our forever home. 

I can't stop crying. We loved this house, we loved this home. It was where we
lived, where we felt safe, where we could be who we were. Now it's going to be
another anonymous shell in a world of empty shells. 

I am so sorry love, I can't stay. 


I am so sorry.
