## 4th November, 2026

Thankfully it was only a small cold, not the flu. I genuinely don't know if I'd
survive if I got the flu. Now I feel better but I need to rethink my priorities.
I had been assuming that I was alone, so there were no diseases, but honestly
no, that can't be the case, I've clearly still got my gut microbiome, I bet if
I had a microscope I'd see dermodex on my face. I know viruses still exist, but
if they're even alive or not is a matter for debate. 


No, actually it's not. I'm the ultimate authority, they're alive. 

But yeah, while I've not _seen_ any animals since it happened, that doesn't
mean they didn't exist. Hell, some of the hallucinations from that bloody spice
might have been real. 

Still, today is just gentle recuperation and relaxing. Doesn't hurt that it's
apparently fog season out there. Technically the sun's up but you can only tell
that by the way the fog is a bit brighter in one spot.
