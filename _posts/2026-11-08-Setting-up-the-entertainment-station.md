## 8th October 2026

Well, the nights are drawing in and the time is coming where I will need to save
heat, keep myself happy and live well. 

So I've set up multiple tents, one for cooking with an induction hob, I have the
power now, I can afford to cook a hot meal every day. 

I have a clean house and I have a schedule to keep it clean. Hard to do in
practice, but if I remember one day in three, I'm already ahead of the game.

I've brought a load of stuff from the lorry. Chocolates, vitamins, dvds,
blu-rays, CDs, books. I'm going to have enough entertainment. 

I've set up poly tunnels in the garden. Bye bye lawn, hello winter greens. And
I've set up a poly tunnel to the poly tunnel so I can get there without getting
too wet. I've got water butts on all the drain pipes planned, but putting up
that many tents was enough work for one day. 

This will be the hardest winter, from next year I'm going to have a plan and
a fallback. I'll be able to really live then. This is just time to recover and
time to plan.
