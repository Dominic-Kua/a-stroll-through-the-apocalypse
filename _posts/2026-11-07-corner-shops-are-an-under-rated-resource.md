## 7th October, 2026


So I called in at the corner shop round where the supermarket is, not far from
where I used to live. Because you never know what those little shops will have
in. Well let me tell you, there were a lot of drugs under the counter. There was
also a lot of very illegal looking fireworks in the back room. A lot of probably
knock off alcohol too. 

Basically there's probably a treasure trove of things that could be useful all
over the show. So I have enough fireworks to see me through the year, or even
beyond. 

I should go into the city and get the most luxury Christmas tree I can. Advent
starts on the 29th I think this year. Might put it up early though, not like
anyone's here to judge me.
