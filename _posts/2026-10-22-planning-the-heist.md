# 22nd October, 2026

Well if I want to maximise this trip to the warehouse I need to be smart.
I don't want to waste all the fuel or charge I can get going back and forth, so
I need a shopping list. I need to know exactly what I want. I'll be good to go
back in say, March, April? So let's assume April, which means I need enough to
survive for five months. Call it 6 months for a real buffer. That means cooking
supplies for at least 182 x 3 meals, dried food, water filters. I've got
a sleeping bag, but I should probably get some bathing fuel too. Cleanliness is
next to godliness after all. 

Disease is a real issue with the number of strays around, rats, mice etc. The
world is teeming now. Birds were very loud this morning. 

I can't believe it's just me and the animals. 

Anyway I'll also pick up all the dried food I can get and hope to be able to
rehydrate it. I don't have to worry about a frost for a while so I can store
water outside at least. 

Maybe I should get some way to wash my clothes, I can't just keep wearing new
ones, even if it feels so good.
