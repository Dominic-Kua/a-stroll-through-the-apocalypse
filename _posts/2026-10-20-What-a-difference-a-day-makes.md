# 20th of October, 2026

It's amazing what a night in clean sheets and a building full of toilets with
one flush left in them will do for you. Shame there was no chance of the all you
can eat breakfast. You know I'll probably never have black pudding ever again. 


It's funny, the stories all said that the apocalypse would have us all hunting,
farming, eating fungi in the Moscow Metro. But in the end, it's just me and
there's more food than I can possibly ever eat all around me. Now I'm out of the
shock, maybe I could go travelling. It's not like I can't just get a car,
probably enough fuel to drive forever out there in the petrol station tanks, if
I can get it out. Or electric, so much solar was installed these last few years,
I can probably just charge it from house sockets. Not like I'm in a rush. 


Maybe I can drive through the chunnel, maybe set up a winter house on the med. 

Hey, that's actually not a bad idea, I've got the whole world to play with,
Given I don't have doctors any more, I guess I'll have maybe twenty, thirty
years left? I could read everything... The British Library is still there for
me. Oh now I need to go to London. This could be a very nice apocalypse after
all!


Right, first of all, finish filling the van up and head back home. Home for now,
maybe I'll live in a mansion instead, though heating and power I guess. Well
I can definitely do better than a two bed semi in the sticks.
