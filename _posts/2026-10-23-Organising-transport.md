## 22nd October, 2026

So I know what I want to get, but I've been thinking about this all wrong.
Taking one van is short term thinking. I've remembered something, those
warehouses are not ordered and organised. Everything is everywhere. I'm going to
need to spend ages there to make any progress. It'll probably be easier to do
a recce tomorrow and then see if there's somewhere there I can set up in. Why
bring everything back here if everything I need is there? There's a campervan
two streets over I could take. That'd be easy to keep fuelled and keep me warm. 

Anyway I'll drive up there and have a look see. I've plotted a route, should
only take me an hour or so at most if the roads are clear. Some have cars all
over them. If that happens I'll be slower. Upside, even if it takes me two hours
each way, I can easily do it in a day. I've set an alarm for tomorrow morning
and I've broken into my stash of protein bars to have road snacks. 

Honestly, I have a load of dried food and fuel now. Might try a few days of the
old dried food. 

Fun fact, oat milk is shelf stable, I made a little trip to the supermarket
again and picked up thirty litres, should do me a while. You do need to
refrigerate it after opening, but I guess the upside of a cold winter would be
food storage?

The canned food is just boring now.

Wish me luck for tomorrow, I ride with the dawn!
