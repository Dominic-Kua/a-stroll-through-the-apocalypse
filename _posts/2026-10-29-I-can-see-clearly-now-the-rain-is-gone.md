## 29th October, 2026

Wow, that was a big storm. It lasted for nearly 2 whole days and barely any let
up. The roads are flooded, I still can't leave. I've got about as much as I want
in the lorry now, but I suspect I will have to go and hunt in the stacks for
some more equipment. No doubt there's trees down, and no one else is out here to
clear them but me. 

You know sometimes I really miss society. 

That said, no one has thrown a can in my hedge in months, so it's not all bad. 


I'll look for some ropes saws and whatever else I can find. I basically have to
be able to do everything now. I should see about getting a kit together with the
essentials. I was in scouts forty years ago, I'm sure I can remember enough to
get started. 

Maybe I can find a book. Though honestly this place is such a mess, I've been
here for days and I've not really gone further than about ten metres in any
direction. How many more of these places are there? 

The ai bot came back out today, it seems to be set to only have children's
stories and talk in baby talk. I need a better one, but it's so good to just say
hello and hear something reply.
