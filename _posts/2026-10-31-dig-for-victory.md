## 31st October, 2026

All saint's night, hallowe'en, I'm not dressing up, but if you don't tell
anyone, I won't. I've realised that I will probably enjoy some non-canned food
in the future. Don't get me wrong, It's amazing what they put in cans, and now
I'm trying stay healthy again, it's also quite impressive how much fibre there
is in beans. 

I won't lie to you, dear diary, I had been neglecting my fibre intake, but one
can of beans a day and, well, that's not a problem any more. 

Still there's not going to be any fresh meat in my future, so I might as well go
and grow some veg. There's a garden centre just down the road, they'll have
seeds. I can grow tomatoes, chillies, herbs, and fresh greens inside. Outside
onions, potatoes, cabbages. You know how much I miss chips? That's something
that did _not_ survive the canning process. 

I'll use some dehydrated soy mince and make burgers. I do wish my hallucinated
chickens were real though. Did a bit of a search and yeah, that "spice" wasn't
the only thing he had. I must have been just slightly tripping for months. Don't
know if it helped or not, but it was a weird time and yeah, maybe I needed to
not be here in the moment.
