## 6th November, 2026

Well there's some sunshine today at least, that bonfire last night was
excellent, might have slightly burnt some of the garage of the house down the
street, but it rained and put it out again, so it's all good. Those sparks went
a long way. 

The fireworks were fun too, a couple didn't go off, but I've seen enough
educational videos that I didn't go back to them. They were rained on all night
so it should be fine. 

But the skies have cleared and I'm in need of some more supplies, things
I didn't think I'd need. But I should be able to get them all at the supermarket
instead of going back to the warehouse. 


Honestly I have like twenty fireworks left over, I might set some off tonight.
The silence, it gets to me you know? No people, no animals, no birdsong. 

The silence tells me I'm alone. I don't like that.

Nothing says humanity is here like an explosion or two. You know I should set
off one a night, just for me. If it's good weather tomorrow, I'll get back to
the warehouse, they probably have crates of them there.
