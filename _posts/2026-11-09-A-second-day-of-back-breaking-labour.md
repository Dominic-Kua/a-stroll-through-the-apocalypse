## 9th October 2026


Well it's Monday so back to work. Got so much done today, but I am definitely
taking tomorrow off.

I actually carted a load of soil into the poly tunnels and... kinda put it on
top of the lawn. Then I put the winter spinach seeds in and some salad seeds in
too. Watered it all thoroughly. Then I changed my shoes and put down some carpet
from next door, that I had to cut up. So now I have a carpeted lawn. Waiting for
it to dry before I try and fix that one.

Turns out raised beds are a good idea, big piles of wet soil are not so good. 

Fresh off the back of that rousing success, I actually read the instructions and
put up four more butts on my house and next door. Now I have about a thousand
litres of water storage for the garden, which I hope will do. Pretty proud of
how they turned out actually.

Then I did something a little fancy, fried spam fritters, instant mashed
potatoes and peas and sweetcorn. Hot and tasty with gravy. Fibre/protein bar for
dessert with a vitamin gummy and I should be getting my nutrition.  

I'm going to get through this. 

I have as much to live for as I can find.
