## 24th November, 2026

I am in the snow again. I had my shower last night and it was heavenly. The
firework was really pretty but about ten minutes after it went off, the snow
started again. I called it. Call me captain meteorology because I got it right,
The temps actually went up again too. We're at just about zero now. Balmy. 


The snow is the thick heavy kind that makes the silence of the world feel
normal. Even before, when the snow was heavy, the sound of the world just
dropped away. 


You know my wife, well she was my girlfriend at the time, once made a big
snowman in the garden. I was there helping, rolling snowballs, making tea. But
after a few hours she had a snowman taller than she was, with a hat, a scarf and
a face made of stones pressed into the snow. 

Those memories are the ones you keep. That day was so lovely. We had nothing to
do and we did it as hard as we could. I miss her so much. 


The next morning however I came downstairs and there had been enough of a thaw
over night that the snowman had melted just enough that his head was sunken in
his body, his smile was now incredibly sinister, like a serial killer in the
movies. And he was about a metre from the kitchen window so I came down for my
coffee in the morning and near enough had a heart attack. 

She laughed at me for years for that. I'd give anything to hear that laugh
again. 

What the hell happened?
