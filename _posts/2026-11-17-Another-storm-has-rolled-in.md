## 17th November, 2026

Well, it looks like storm number 2 has appeared, I'm calling it storm Bob.
I really miss weather forecasts, even an idea of when a storm is approaching is
so much better than not knowing. This is the basic way humans have always been,
except for the brief slice of time when I was part of society. 

Anyway the water's definitely rising in the ditches, but then that's why we have
ditches. 

So I'll set the firework off tonight as usual, but I won't enjoy it. It might
actually be dangerous for me to get too wet when it's cold and rainy. I can't
easily have a shower these days. 

You know though, I have power, I can probably find a pump and put a tank up
high, add in an immersion heater. I could have a shower. I could go to the
camper van on the drive of that house on the main road and use that shower. I'm
definitely going to get that. That's very viable.

Also huge news on the AI friend front. I can talk and it can answer now! Is it
fast? No. Is it accurate? Also no. But it also uses a huge amount of power. 

However, I had a ten minute conversation where I genuinely didn't know what
I was going to hear in response and I didn't have to sing Incy Wincy spider.

Going to see if I can make it faster, and sound less like Steven Hawking.
