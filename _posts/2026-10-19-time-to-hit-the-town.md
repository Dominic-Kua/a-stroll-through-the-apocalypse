## 19th October, 2026

The van still has three quarters of a tank in it, I can easily make it to the
city centre on that. I bet I can get a lot of good stuff there, but maybe not
all at once. Spread it out over a few days, Health is wealth after all and it's
not like money has any meaning any more. Wish me luck!



I am currently in the fanciest hotel in town, looks like they had several rooms
made up and empty and sealed pretty well. So I've got clean sheets, a kingsize
bed and, get this, I had forgotten that there was a second hand electronics
store. I've got four more portable blu-ray players, yes not just DVDs any more!
I've got about 300 dvds and blu-rays to add to my collection. I've got solar
lamps, I've got thermal sleeping bags, I've got chemical warming pads. I've got
a camp stove and enough gas for months. 

But better than all of that is the brand new, ready to sell, plug-in solar
panels and batteries. I can set all the houses on the street up as battery
chargers. Even in the dead of winter I'm going to be warm and cosy. 

I honestly think I can have an enjoyable life, even picked up some
multi-vitamins. Don't want scurvy.
