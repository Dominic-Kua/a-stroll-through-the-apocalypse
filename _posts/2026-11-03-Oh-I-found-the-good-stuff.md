## 3rd November 2026

So I did drag myself to the pharmacy for those tissues in the end, and some
cream for my nose so I wasn't just greasy all the time. While I was there
I noticed a little safe I hadn't seen before. 

Now I want to say I have amazing safe cracking skills, but the notebook on the
counter had the combination written in the back leaf, so yeah, physical security
fail there. Why anyone could walk in and notice that, then get access to some
_really_ good drugs. 


I still have the cold, but I also don't care any more. Wintery out, I've set up
the tent in the spare bedroom, so I have double insulation and you know what?
It's lovely. Thermometer says twenty two degrees and I don't even have a heater
on, just me. I set it up around one of the lovely mattresses I brought back and
now I'm lounging in a warm nest, drinking hot chocolate and watching TV. 

This apocalypse business isn't so bad, you know? 


I know I still need to plan for next year, but I really need to get better
first.
