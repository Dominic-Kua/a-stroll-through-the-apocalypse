## 22nd November, 2026

OK now it's snowing, which might be good news? It's definitely warmed up, but
that's strictly relative. It's still five below, but it's warmer than it was. My
big worry now is that when it thaws, there's going to be a big flood as this
snow melts. 

I've already got my tent on a pile of yoga mats up in the spare bedroom, I've
moved a chemical toilet up there too, because I am _not_ going out into the snow
to poop. A man has standards. 

Felix has been shut down for the moment, the drain on the batteries is intense,
they weren't made for this I don't think. Basically they're lasting about half
as long as expected. I've brought 2 into the tent, big ones, up to 10kWh of
power in them. This double walled, insulated floor set up for the tents has kept
temperatures in here up above freezing, but not by much. I'm wearing everything
i have at once it feels like. The camper van should be OK at least. Putting
water in it was a pain so my last shower ran it dry. You know the moment it's
above zero out there, I'm going right to that van, carrying as much water and
power and gas as I can and I'm having a really long shower. 


I was not ready for this. It's never this cold here. I was expecting a little
colder than normal, because the urban heat island effect wouldn't be happening,
but I think the combination of arctic winds, the flooding and the fact that no
one but me is putting out any heat have affected it. Did the biology of animals
cause so much heat? 


Wait, if all the animals went away, why didn't the meat in the cans? Were there
corpses out there? If it only took the living... Are you alive out there? I miss
you. I wish I understood why this was happening.
