17th of October, 2026

Yesterday I was full of hope, full of life. But today is a different story, we
have an allotted time on this Earth and I should be glad that mine has lasted
longer than everyone else's I guess. 

But it's hard to have gratitude when your back aches like you're never going to
get out of the house again. I guess a few months sat around, eating out of cans
and watching DVDs on a portable player didn't do my physical health any good.
Though in my defence, I was struggling with the fact that everyone else is gone
and I will never talk to another human again in my life. I'm just one man,
I cannot make more people. 

Still I was smart enough to pick up all the medicine I could so at least I have
painkillers. Guess I need to get back in shape if I'm going to make the most of
this life of mine. 

I bet that warehouse has loads of exercise equipment.
