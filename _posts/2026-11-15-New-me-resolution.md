## 15th November, 2026

Well I have to have a goal, a human without a reason to live rapidly stops
living. 

So here's what I'll do: 

1) I will keep setting off a firework every night at 20:00. Even if it's
miserable, it's a sign I'm alive.

2) I will actually use one of the very stupidly powerful laptops I found in
a software developer's house and I will write a conversation partner for me. I'm
ahead of the game here, there's an AI already on it, and I can tell it to write
me the bit that talks and listens. 

3) I am going to grab an encyclopaedia, an atlas and work out what the best
place for me to live actually is. Because the swamp is clearly untenable. It's
going to have to have a lot of land not prone to flooding, but also where I can
grow food. It has to have a big online warehouse or two in easy driving
distance. It has to have a nice climate, and damn it a pool. 


That first one keeps me in touch with the world. 

The second one will hopefully keep me sane, sorry dear diary, but you're not
exactly chatty. 

The third one will give me a nice life. And it's not just a where's closest.
I can literally go anywhere. If I need to learn to sail a boat, why not? Nothing
but time. I could go across the channel, I could drive across to Asia. Hell
I could settle in India if I wanted. Even Australia is about 74% less terrifying
now there's no snakes or spiders.
