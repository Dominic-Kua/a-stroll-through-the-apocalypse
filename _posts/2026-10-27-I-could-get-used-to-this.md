## 27th October, 2026

A second night sleeping in style, there's even beds I could put together here,
but I won't. The whole place is gloomy, but I've found a big tent, the kind from
a fair or something and set it up next to the loading dock. I've got batteries,
more outside charging from Solar, I've got food, drinks, lights, tv, a game
console... Yeah regular home away from home. I should definitely leave this set
up so I can enjoy the "hotel" life. 

Come to think of it, I should go to the seaside next year, get myself a holiday
home. If I can work out how to get across the channel, I could even have
a winter home on the med. 


I might be alone but that's no reason to live a sad life I guess?

On the lorry front, I've got a load more put in there. Probably about a quarter
full now. I realised I need to somehow stop it all from rolling around in there
so I've got as many batteries as I can find and they'll be the barriers I put
everything else in. Maybe one more day, stock up on, ironically, stock cubes and
vitamins and I would really like wood burning stove. I know I live in a smoke
free zone, but you know what, if they catch me I'll just enjoy talking to them. 

That Ai bot thing is... crap.

Need to find a better one. I did snag an abolute top end laptop though. And
another tent, never know when I'll want another base.
