## 21st November, 2026

I went down to the river today, I've seen it freeze before, but only around the
edges, the weir itself is starting to freeze. It's only November. The
thermometer is reading eight below zero, it's getting colder throughout the day.
My batteries are starting to drain really fast now. I've brought as many of the
portable ones in that I can, and stolen every quilt in the street to wrap around
them. I hope they stay warm enough. 

The good news is that the clear dark skies at night are meaning the firework
looks amazing. I nearly gave myself hypothermia last night just looking at the
stars. It's incredible how much you can see now that it's truly dark. Well
except for a big bright old moon. I wonder if the space stations still have
people on? 

I mean did this only affect Earth, or was it all animals, everywhere? 

This would be a thousand times easier if my wife was still here. Why did you
leave me? I miss you so much. I can talk with Felix, but I'm never excited to
show him what I've discovered like I was with you. 

Come back to me, please.
